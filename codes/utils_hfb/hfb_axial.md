# Axially Symmetric Hartree–Fock–Bogoliubov Method

### Symmetry Assumptions

This note specializes the general [Hartree–Fock–Bogoliubov Method](hfb.md) to an even-even nucleus without quasiparticle blocking.

##### Axial Symmetry

Rotations about the $z$ axis are generated by

$$
\hat R_z(\varphi)=e^{-i\varphi\hat J_z/\hbar}.
$$

In a basis with definite angular-momentum projection,

$$
\hat J_z=\hbar\sum_\alpha\Omega_\alpha\hat c_\alpha^*\hat c_\alpha,\qquad \Omega_\alpha=\pm\frac12,\pm\frac32,\ldots.
$$

Therefore,

$$
[\hat J_z,\hat c_\alpha^*]=\hbar\Omega_\alpha\hat c_\alpha^*,\qquad [\hat J_z,\hat c_\alpha]=-\hbar\Omega_\alpha\hat c_\alpha.
$$

The elementary particle-hole and pair-creation operators satisfy

$$
[\hat J_z,\hat c_\alpha^*\hat c_\beta]=\hbar(\Omega_\alpha-\Omega_\beta)\hat c_\alpha^*\hat c_\beta,
$$

and

$$
[\hat J_z,\hat c_\alpha^*\hat c_\beta^*]=\hbar(\Omega_\alpha+\Omega_\beta)\hat c_\alpha^*\hat c_\beta^*.
$$

##### Parity Symmetry

Parity conservation requires

$$
\hat\Pi^2=1,\qquad [\hat H,\hat\Pi]=0.
$$

In a basis with definite parity,

$$
\hat\Pi|\phi_\alpha\rangle=\pi_\alpha|\phi_\alpha\rangle,\qquad \pi_\alpha=\pm1.
$$

Therefore,

$$
[\hat\Pi,\hat c_\alpha^*]=(\pi_\alpha-1)\hat c_\alpha^*\hat\Pi,\qquad [\hat\Pi,\hat c_\alpha]=(\pi_\alpha-1)\hat c_\alpha\hat\Pi.
$$

The elementary particle-hole and pair-creation operators satisfy

$$
[\hat\Pi,\hat c_\alpha^*\hat c_\beta]=(\pi_\alpha\pi_\beta-1)\hat c_\alpha^*\hat c_\beta\hat\Pi,
$$

and

$$
[\hat\Pi,\hat c_\alpha^*\hat c_\beta^*]=(\pi_\alpha\pi_\beta-1)\hat c_\alpha^*\hat c_\beta^*\hat\Pi.
$$

##### Opposite-$\Omega$ States

For each state in the $\Omega>0$ sector, define

$$
|\phi_{\bar\alpha}\rangle\equiv|\phi_{-\alpha}\rangle,\qquad \Omega_{\bar\alpha}=-\Omega_\alpha,\qquad \pi_{\bar\alpha}=\pi_\alpha.
$$

This labeling does not assume time-reversal invariance.

Axial symmetry gives

$$
(\Omega_\alpha-\Omega_\beta)h_{\alpha\beta}=(\Omega_\alpha-\Omega_\beta)\rho_{\alpha\beta}=0,\qquad(\Omega_\alpha+\Omega_\beta)\Delta_{\alpha\beta}=(\Omega_\alpha+\Omega_\beta)\kappa_{\alpha\beta}=0.
$$

For $\alpha,\beta$ in the $\Omega>0$ sector,

$$
h_{\alpha\bar\beta}=h_{\bar\alpha\beta}=\rho_{\alpha\bar\beta}=\rho_{\bar\alpha\beta}=0,\qquad \Delta_{\alpha\beta}=\Delta_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}=\kappa_{\alpha\beta}=\kappa_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}=0.
$$

Parity conservation further gives

$$
\pi_\alpha\neq\pi_\beta\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_{\alpha\beta}=\rho_{\alpha\beta}=\Delta_{\alpha\bar\beta}=\kappa_{\alpha\bar\beta}=0.
$$

### Axial HFB Equation

##### HFB Block Structure

Let $\{|\phi_\alpha\rangle\}$ span the $\Omega>0$ sector. The opposite-$\Omega$ labeling does not assume time-reversal invariance. In the ordered basis $(|\phi_\alpha\rangle,|\phi_{\bar\alpha}\rangle)$,

$$
h=\begin{pmatrix}h_{\alpha\beta}&h_{\alpha\bar\beta}\\h_{\bar\alpha\beta}&h_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}\end{pmatrix},\qquad \Delta=\begin{pmatrix}\Delta_{\alpha\beta}&\Delta_{\alpha\bar\beta}\\\Delta_{\bar\alpha\beta}&\Delta_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}\end{pmatrix},
$$

with

$$
h^\dagger=h,\qquad \Delta^T=-\Delta.
$$

The generalized Hamiltonian is

$$
\mathcal H=\begin{pmatrix}h_{\alpha\beta}-\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}&h_{\alpha\bar\beta}&\Delta_{\alpha\beta}&\Delta_{\alpha\bar\beta}\\h_{\bar\alpha\beta}&h_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}-\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}&\Delta_{\bar\alpha\beta}&\Delta_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}\\-\Delta_{\alpha\beta}^*&-\Delta_{\alpha\bar\beta}^*&-h_{\alpha\beta}^*+\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}&-h_{\alpha\bar\beta}^*\\-\Delta_{\bar\alpha\beta}^*&-\Delta_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}^*&-h_{\bar\alpha\beta}^*&-h_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}^*+\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Axial and parity selection rules give

$$
\mathcal H_{\Omega\pi}=\begin{pmatrix}h_{\alpha\beta}-\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}&0&0&\Delta_{\alpha\bar\beta}\\0&h_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}-\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}&\Delta_{\bar\alpha\beta}&0\\0&-\Delta_{\alpha\bar\beta}^*&-h_{\alpha\beta}^*+\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}&0\\-\Delta_{\bar\alpha\beta}^*&0&0&-h_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}^*+\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}\end{pmatrix},
$$

Matrix entries retain the row index $\alpha$ before the column index $\beta$, with bars indicating the opposite-$\Omega$ sector.

The axial HFB equation is

$$
\mathcal H_{\Omega\pi}\begin{pmatrix}U_\mu^+\\U_\mu^-\\V_\mu^+\\V_\mu^-\end{pmatrix}=E_\mu\begin{pmatrix}U_\mu^+\\U_\mu^-\\V_\mu^+\\V_\mu^-\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The $(U_\mu^+,V_\mu^-)$ and $(U_\mu^-,V_\mu^+)$ sectors decouple without assuming time-reversal invariance.

##### Solutions of the HFB Block Equations

The axial HFB equation separates into two blocks:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H^+\equiv\begin{pmatrix}h_{\alpha\beta}-\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}&\Delta_{\alpha\bar\beta}\\-\Delta_{\bar\alpha\beta}^*&-h_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}^*+\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}\end{pmatrix},\qquad \mathcal H^-\equiv\begin{pmatrix}h_{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}-\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}&\Delta_{\bar\alpha\beta}\\-\Delta_{\alpha\bar\beta}^*&-h_{\alpha\beta}^*+\lambda\delta_{\alpha\beta}\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

For each $(\Omega,\pi)$ block with $\Omega>0$, solve

$$
\mathcal H^+X_\mu^+=E_\mu^+X_\mu^+,\qquad \mathcal H^-X_\nu^-=E_\nu^-X_\nu^-.
$$

we can solve the two blocks independently. The corresponding quasiparticle amplitudes are

$$
X_\mu^+\equiv\begin{pmatrix}U_\mu^+\\V_\mu^-\end{pmatrix},\qquad X_\nu^-\equiv\begin{pmatrix}U_\nu^-\\V_\nu^+\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The two sectors decouple without assuming time-reversal invariance. Retain the normalized positive-energy solutions from both sectors.

With particle rows ordered as $(\alpha,\bar\alpha)$ and quasiparticle columns as $(+,-)$, the full Bogoliubov matrices are

$$
\boxed{U=\begin{pmatrix}U^+&0\\0&U^-\end{pmatrix},\qquad V=\begin{pmatrix}0&V^+\\V^-&0\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

The corresponding quasiparticle creation operators are

$$
\hat\beta_\mu^{+*}=\sum_\alpha\left(U_{\alpha\mu}^+\hat c_\alpha^*+V_{\alpha\mu}^-\hat c_{\bar\alpha}\right),
$$

$$
\hat\beta_\nu^{-*}=\sum_\alpha\left(U_{\alpha\nu}^-\hat c_{\bar\alpha}^*+V_{\alpha\nu}^+\hat c_\alpha\right).
$$

No time-reversal relation between $U^+,V^-$ and $U^-,V^+$ has been assumed.

##### Density Blocks

Using the Bogoliubov matrices defined in [Solutions of the HFB Block Equations](#solutions-of-the-hfb-block-equations),

$$
\rho=V^*V^T,\qquad \kappa=V^*U^T.
$$

The normal density preserves the axial sectors:

$$
\boxed{\rho=\begin{pmatrix}(V^+)^*(V^+)^T&0\\0&(V^-)^*(V^-)^T\end{pmatrix}\equiv\begin{pmatrix}\rho^+&0\\0&\rho^-\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

The pairing tensor connects opposite axial sectors:

$$
\boxed{\kappa=\begin{pmatrix}0&(V^+)^*(U^-)^T\\(V^-)^*(U^+)^T&0\end{pmatrix}\equiv\begin{pmatrix}0&\kappa^{+-}\\\kappa^{-+}&0\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

The antisymmetry $\kappa^T=-\kappa$ gives

$$
\kappa^{-+}=-(\kappa^{+-})^T.
$$

Thus, $\rho$ preserves $(\Omega,\pi)$, while $\kappa$ connects $(\Omega,\pi)$ with $(-\Omega,\pi)$. No time-reversal relation between $\rho^+$ and $\rho^-$ has been assumed.
