耦合道形式理论

体系与通道基

\[P(J^{\pi_P})\rightarrow D(I^{\pi_I})+\alpha(0^+;l)\qquad c=\{l,I\}.\]
\[\mathcal Y_{JM}^{(c)} =\big[Y_l\otimes\Phi_I\big]_{JM}\Phi_\alpha =\sum_{m,M_I}\langle lm;IM_I|JM\rangle Y_{lm}(\hat{\mathbf r})\Phi_{IM_I}(\xi_D)\Phi_\alpha(\xi_\alpha)\]
\[\Psi_{JM}^{(c)}(\mathbf r,\xi_D,\xi_\alpha) =\frac{u_c(r)}{r}\mathcal Y_{JM}^{(c)}(\hat{\mathbf r},\xi_D,\xi_\alpha),\qquad \Psi_{JM}=\sum_c a^{(c)}\Psi_{JM}^{(c)}.\]

哈密顿量

\[\hat H=\hat H_D+\hat H_\alpha+\hat T_r+\hat V({\bf r}).\]
\[V_{cc'}^{JM}(r) =\left\langle\mathcal Y_{JM}^{(c)} \middle|\hat V({\bf r})\middle| \mathcal Y_{JM}^{(c')}\right\rangle_{\hat{\mathbf r},\xi_D,\xi_\alpha},\qquad E_c\equiv E-E_I-E_\alpha.\]
\[\boxed{ \sum_{c'}\left[ -\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu} \left(\frac{d^2}{dr^2}-\frac{l_c(l_c+1)}{r^2}\right)\delta_{cc'} +V_{cc'}^{JM}(r) \right]u_{c'}(r) =E_cu_c(r) }.\]

耦合道形式理论

相互作用与通道矩阵元

\[\hat V({\bf r})=\sum_{\lambda\mu}\hat V_{\lambda\mu}(r)Y_{\lambda\mu}^{*}(\hat{\mathbf r})=\sum_{\lambda\mu}(-1)^\mu\hat V_{\lambda\mu}(r)Y_{\lambda,-\mu}(\hat{\mathbf r}).\]
\[V_{cc'}^{JM}(r)=\Big\langle\big[Y_l\otimes\Phi_I\big]_{JM}\Phi_\alpha\Big|\hat V({\bf r})\Big|\big[Y_{l'}\otimes\Phi_{I'}\big]_{JM}\Phi_\alpha\Big\rangle.\]
\[V_{cc'}^{JM}(r)=\sum_{\lambda\mu}\sum_{mM_I}\sum_{m'M_I'}(-1)^\mu\langle lm;IM_I|JM\rangle\langle l'm';I'M_I'|JM\rangle\big\langle lm\big|Y_{\lambda,-\mu}\big|l'm'\big\rangle\Big\langle\Phi_{IM_I}\Phi_\alpha\Big|\hat V_{\lambda\mu}(r)\Big|\Phi_{I'M_I'}\Phi_\alpha\Big\rangle.\]

约化矩阵元

\[\Big\langle\Phi_{IM_I}\Phi_\alpha\Big|\hat V_{\lambda\mu}(r)\Big|\Phi_{I'M_I'}\Phi_\alpha\Big\rangle=(-1)^{I-M_I}\begin{pmatrix}I&\lambda&I'\\-M_I&\mu&M_I'\end{pmatrix}\Big\langle\Phi_I\Phi_\alpha\Big\|\hat V_\lambda(r)\Big\|\Phi_{I'}\Phi_\alpha\Big\rangle.\]
\[\big\langle lm\big|Y_{\lambda,-\mu}\big|l'm'\big\rangle=(-1)^{l-m}\begin{pmatrix}l&\lambda&l'\\-m&-\mu&m'\end{pmatrix}\big\langle l\big\|Y_\lambda\big\|l'\big\rangle.\]
\[\boxed{V_{cc'}^{JM}(r)=\sum_\lambda(-1)^{l'+I+J}\begin{Bmatrix}l&I&J\\I'&l'&\lambda\end{Bmatrix}\big\langle l\big\|Y_\lambda\big\|l'\big\rangle\Big\langle\Phi_I\Phi_\alpha\Big\|\hat V_\lambda(r)\Big\|\Phi_{I'}\Phi_\alpha\Big\rangle}.\]

示意图

从内禀系到实验室系的变换

\[\cos\theta'=\hat{\mathbf r}'\cdot\hat{\mathbf z}'=\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}_D.\]
\[\hat V'(\mathbf r')=\sum_\lambda\hat V'_{\lambda0}(r)Y_{\lambda0}(\hat{\mathbf r}')=\sum_\lambda\hat V'_{\lambda0}(r)\sqrt{\frac{2\lambda+1}{4\pi}}P_\lambda(\cos\theta').\]
\[P_\lambda(\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}_D)=\frac{4\pi}{2\lambda+1}\sum_\mu Y_{\lambda\mu}^{*}(\hat{\mathbf r})Y_{\lambda\mu}(\hat{\mathbf n}_D).\]
\[\hat V'(\mathbf r') =\sum_{\lambda,\mu} \left[ \sqrt{\frac{4\pi}{2\lambda+1}} \hat V'_{\lambda0}(r) Y_{\lambda\mu}(\hat{\mathbf n}_D) \right] Y_{\lambda\mu}^{*}(\hat{\mathbf r}).\]
\[\hat V(\mathbf r)=\hat V'(\mathbf r')=\sum_{\lambda\mu}\hat V_{\lambda\mu}(r)Y_{\lambda\mu}^{*}(\hat{\mathbf r})=\sum_\lambda\hat V'_{\lambda0}(r)Y_{\lambda0}(\hat{\mathbf r}').\]
\[\boxed{\hat V_{\lambda\mu}(r)=\hat V'_{\lambda0}(r)D_{\mu0}^{\lambda *}(\Omega_D)}.\]

耦合道形式理论

内禀系约化矩阵元

\[\Phi_{IM_IK}(\Omega_D,\eta_D)=\sqrt{\frac{2I+1}{16\pi^2}}\left[D_{M_IK}^{I*}(\Omega_D)\chi_K(\eta_D)+(-1)^{I+K}D_{M_I,-K}^{I*}(\Omega_D)\chi_{-K}(\eta_D)\right].\]
\[\Big\langle\Phi_{IM_IK}\Phi_\alpha\Big|\hat V_{\lambda\mu}(r)\Big|\Phi_{I'M_I'K}\Phi_\alpha\Big\rangle=\frac{\hat I\hat I'}{2}(-1)^{M_I-K}\begin{pmatrix}I&\lambda&I'\\M_I&-\mu&-M_I'\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}I&\lambda&I'\\K&0&-K\end{pmatrix}\left[V'_{\lambda0;K,K}(r)+(-1)^\lambda V'_{\lambda0;-K,-K}(r)\right].\]
若只取 \(\lambda\) 为偶数：
\[\Big\langle\Phi_{IM_IK}\Phi_\alpha\Big|\hat V_{\lambda\mu}(r)\Big|\Phi_{I'M_I'K}\Phi_\alpha\Big\rangle=\hat I\hat I'(-1)^{I-M_I}\begin{pmatrix}I&\lambda&I'\\-M_I&\mu&M_I'\end{pmatrix}(-1)^{I-K}\begin{pmatrix}I&\lambda&I'\\-K&0&K\end{pmatrix}V'_{\lambda0;K,K}(r).\]
\[\boxed{\Big\langle IK\Big\|\hat V_\lambda(r)\Big\|I'K\Big\rangle=\hat I\hat I'(-1)^{I-K}\begin{pmatrix}I&\lambda&I'\\-K&0&K\end{pmatrix}V'_{\lambda0;K,K}(r)}.\]

当前计算结果

库仑耦合

\[V^{\prime C}(\mathbf r')=\frac{e^2Z_\alpha Z_D}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\int d^3r_D'\,\frac{\rho_D(\mathbf r_D')}{|\mathbf r'-\mathbf r_D'|}.\]
\[\frac{1}{|\mathbf r'-\mathbf r_D'|}=\sum_{\lambda\mu}\frac{4\pi}{2\lambda+1}\frac{r_<^\lambda}{r_>^{\lambda+1}}Y_{\lambda\mu}^{*}(\hat{\mathbf r}')Y_{\lambda\mu}(\hat{\mathbf r}_D').\]
\[Q_{\lambda\mu}^D=eZ_D\int d^3r_D'\,\rho_D(\mathbf r_D')r_D^\lambda Y_{\lambda\mu}(\hat{\mathbf r}_D').\]
\[V^{\prime C}(\mathbf r')=\frac{eZ_\alpha}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\sum_{\lambda\mu}\frac{4\pi}{2\lambda+1}\frac{Q_{\lambda\mu}^D}{r^{\lambda+1}}Y_{\lambda\mu}^{*}(\hat{\mathbf r}').\]

核势耦合

\[\hat R_D(\hat{\mathbf r}',\xi_D)=R_0^D\left[1+\sum_{\lambda\mu}\hat a_{\lambda\mu}(\xi_D)Y_{\lambda\mu}^{*}(\hat{\mathbf r}')\right].\]
\[\Big\langle\Phi_{IK}\Big\|\hat R_{D,\lambda}\Big\|\Phi_{I'K'}\Big\rangle=\hat I\hat I'(-1)^{I-K}\begin{pmatrix}I&\lambda&I'\\-K&0&K'\end{pmatrix}\Big\langle\chi_K\Big|\hat R'_{D,\lambda0}\Big|\chi_{K'}\Big\rangle.\]
\[\Big\langle\chi_K\Big|\hat R'_{D,\lambda0}\Big|\chi_{K'}\Big\rangle=\delta_{KK'}R_0^D\left[\sqrt{4\pi}\,\delta_{\lambda0}+\beta_{\lambda0}^D\right].\]
\[\hat V^{\prime N}(\mathbf r')=\frac{V_0}{1+\exp\!\left(\left[r-R_0^\alpha-\hat R_D(\hat{\mathbf r}',\xi_D)\right]/a\right)}.\]

总结与展望

阶段性总结

  • 学习 \(\alpha\)-衰变的耦合道形式理论与从内禀系到实验室系的变换。
  • 构造库仑耦合与核势耦合矩阵元，并代入多通道径向薛定谔方程。
  • 初步复现数值求解流程，获得 \(\alpha\)-衰变分支比与半衰期计算结果。

下一步工作

  • 结合投影壳模型，引入角动量投影与 \(K\) 混合，改进当前耦合道方法中固定 \(K\) 的近似处理；
  • 将现有理论框架推广至奇 \(A\) 核和奇奇核，发展相应的耦合道计算方法。
