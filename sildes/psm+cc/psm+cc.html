生成坐标方法的引入

壳模型

  • 说明：混合多体组态，统一描述单粒子与集体运动。
  • 缺陷：重形变核组态空间庞大，有效哈密顿量难确定。
  • E. Caurier et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 77, 427 (2005); S. E. Koonin et al., Phys. Rep. 278, 1 (1997). K. Hara and Y. Sun, Int. J. Mod. Phys. E 4, 637 (1995).

平均场方法

  • 说明：以优化的单个参考态，近似描述重形变核基态。
  • 缺陷：描述集体涨落与激发谱，需要超越静态平均场。
  • P. Ring and P. Schuck, The Nuclear Many-Body Problem, Springer (2004). M. Bender et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 75, 121 (2003); 孟杰等，《物理学进展》31，199 (2011).

生成坐标方法

  • 说明：叠加非正交参考态，结合投影描述低激发谱。
  • 缺陷：结果受参考态空间限制，扩大空间增加计算量。
  • D. L. Hill and J. A. Wheeler, Phys. Rev. 89, 1102 (1953); M. Bender and P.-H. Heenen, Phys. Rev. C 78, 024309 (2008). T. R. Rodríguez and J. L. Egido, Phys. Rev. C 81, 064323 (2010).

生成坐标方法的基本形式

多体波函数的展开

\[|\Psi_\alpha\rangle=\int dq\,f_\alpha(q)|\Phi(q)\rangle.\]

变分原理与 Hill–Wheeler 方程

\[\delta_{f_\alpha^*}\left[\langle\Psi_\alpha|\hat H|\Psi_\alpha\rangle-E_\alpha\langle\Psi_\alpha|\Psi_\alpha\rangle\right]=0.\]
\[\int dq'\,\left[H(q,q')-E_\alpha N(q,q')\right]f_\alpha(q')=0.\]
\[H(q,q')=\langle\Phi(q)|\hat H|\Phi(q')\rangle.\]
\[N(q,q')=\langle\Phi(q)|\Phi(q')\rangle.\]

投影壳模型的基本观点

转动不变性与不同转动取向叠加

\[\text{转动不变性：}[\hat H,\hat R(\Omega)]=0,\qquad|\Psi\rangle=\int d\Omega\,F(\Omega)\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi\rangle.\]
\[\frac{\langle\Phi|\hat H|\Phi\rangle}{\langle\Phi|\Phi\rangle} =\frac{\langle\Phi|\hat R^\dagger(\Omega)\hat H\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi\rangle} {\langle\Phi|\hat R^\dagger(\Omega)\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi\rangle}.\]

角动量投影

\[F(\Omega)=\sum_{IMK}\frac{2I+1}{8\pi^2}f^I_{MK}D^{I*}_{MK}(\Omega).\]
\[\hat P^I_{MK}=\frac{2I+1}{8\pi^2}\int d\Omega\,D^{I*}_{MK}(\Omega)\hat R(\Omega) =\sum_\nu|\nu IM\rangle\langle\nu IK|.\]
\[|\Psi\rangle=\sum_{IMK}f^I_{MK}\hat P^I_{MK}|\Phi\rangle.\]

组态与投影态的定义

准粒子真空与多准粒子组态

\[\hat\beta_\mu|\Phi\rangle=0,\qquad\langle\Phi|\Phi\rangle=1.\]
\[|\Phi_\kappa\rangle=\hat\beta_{\mu_1}^{\dagger}\hat\beta_{\mu_2}^{\dagger}\cdots\hat\beta_{\mu_{n_\kappa}}^{\dagger}|\Phi\rangle, \qquad\langle\Phi_\kappa|=\langle\Phi|\hat\beta_{\mu_{n_\kappa}}\cdots\hat\beta_{\mu_2}\hat\beta_{\mu_1}.\]

角动量与粒子数投影态

\[\hat P^N=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi\,e^{i\phi(\hat N-N)},\qquad\hat P^I_{MK}=\frac{2I+1}{16\pi^2}\int d\Omega\,D^{I*}_{MK}(\Omega)\hat R(\Omega).\]
\[\boxed{|\Phi_{M,K;\kappa}^{IN}\rangle=\hat P^I_{MK}\hat P^N|\Phi_\kappa\rangle.}\]

哈密顿量与投影矩阵元

哈密顿量与对称性

\[\hat H=\sum_{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_\beta+\frac14\sum_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}\bar v_{\alpha\beta,\gamma\delta}\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_\beta^\dagger\hat c_\delta\hat c_\gamma.\]
\[[\hat H,\hat R(\Omega)]=0,\qquad[\hat H,\hat N]=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad[\hat H,\hat P^I_{MK}]=0,\qquad[\hat H,\hat P^N]=0.\]

投影基底中的矩阵元

\[\mathcal H^{I;N}_{K_1,\kappa_1;K_2,\kappa_2}\equiv\langle\Phi_{M,K_1;\kappa_1}^{IN}|\hat H|\Phi_{M,K_2;\kappa_2}^{IN}\rangle=\langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|\hat H\hat P^I_{K_1K_2}\hat P^N|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle.\]
\[\mathcal N^{I;N}_{K_1,\kappa_1;K_2,\kappa_2}\equiv\langle\Phi_{M,K_1;\kappa_1}^{IN}|\Phi_{M,K_2;\kappa_2}^{IN}\rangle=\langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|\hat P^I_{K_1K_2}\hat P^N|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle.\]

粒子与准粒子的转动变换

粒子的转动变换

\(\displaystyle D_{\alpha\beta}(\Omega)=\langle\alpha|\hat R(\Omega)|\beta\rangle,\qquad\hat R(\Omega)\hat c_\alpha\hat R^\dagger(\Omega)=\left[D^\dagger(\Omega)\hat{\boldsymbol c}\right]_\alpha=\sum_\beta D_{\beta\alpha}^{*}(\Omega)\hat c_\beta.\)

准粒子的转动变换

\(\displaystyle \hat{\boldsymbol\beta}=U^\dagger\hat{\boldsymbol c}+V^\dagger\hat{\boldsymbol c}^{\dagger}.\)
\(\displaystyle \hat{\boldsymbol\beta}(\Omega)\equiv\hat R(\Omega)\hat{\boldsymbol\beta}\hat R^\dagger(\Omega)=U^\dagger(\Omega)\hat{\boldsymbol c}+V^\dagger(\Omega)\hat{\boldsymbol c}^{\dagger}.\)
\(\displaystyle U(\Omega)=D(\Omega)U,\qquad V(\Omega)=D^*(\Omega)V.\)
\(\displaystyle \mathcal A(\Omega)=U^TU^*(\Omega)+V^TV^*(\Omega),\qquad\mathcal B(\Omega)=U^TV(\Omega)+V^TU(\Omega).\)
\(\displaystyle \boxed{\hat{\boldsymbol\beta}=\mathcal A^*(\Omega)\hat{\boldsymbol\beta}(\Omega)+\mathcal B^*(\Omega)\hat{\boldsymbol\beta}^{\dagger}(\Omega).}\)

广义 Wick 定理

转动后的准粒子真空

\(\displaystyle |\Phi(\Omega)\rangle\equiv\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi\rangle,\qquad n(\Omega)=\langle\Phi|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle,\qquad\hat\beta_\mu(\Omega)|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle=0.\)

广义 Wick 定理

\(\displaystyle \hat{\boldsymbol s}=(\hat s_1,\ldots,\hat s_{2p}),\qquad\hat s_i=\sum_\alpha\left(a_{i\alpha}\hat c_\alpha+b_{i\alpha}\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\right),\qquad S_{ij}(\Omega)\equiv\frac{\langle\Phi|\hat s_i\hat s_j|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle}{n(\Omega)}.\)
\(\displaystyle \boxed{\langle\Phi|\hat{\boldsymbol s}|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle=\langle\Phi|\hat s_1\cdots\hat s_{2p}|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle=n(\Omega)\operatorname{pf}[S(\Omega)].}\)

组态下的矩阵元

\(\displaystyle \langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|=\langle\Phi|\hat\beta_{\mu_{r_1}}\cdots\hat\beta_{\mu_1},\qquad\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle=\hat\beta_{\nu_1}^\dagger(\Omega)\cdots\hat\beta_{\nu_{r_2}}^\dagger(\Omega)|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle.\)
\(\displaystyle \begin{aligned} \langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle&:\quad\hat{\boldsymbol s}=\left(\hat\beta_{\mu_{r_1}},\ldots,\hat\beta_{\mu_1},\hat\beta_{\nu_1}^\dagger(\Omega),\ldots,\hat\beta_{\nu_{r_2}}^\dagger(\Omega)\right).\\ \langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_\beta\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle&:\quad\hat{\boldsymbol s}=\left(\hat\beta_{\mu_{r_1}},\ldots,\hat\beta_{\mu_1},\hat c_\alpha^\dagger,\hat c_\beta,\hat\beta_{\nu_1}^\dagger(\Omega),\ldots,\hat\beta_{\nu_{r_2}}^\dagger(\Omega)\right).\\ \langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_\beta^\dagger\hat c_\delta\hat c_\gamma\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle&:\quad\hat{\boldsymbol s}=\left(\hat\beta_{\mu_{r_1}},\ldots,\hat\beta_{\mu_1},\hat c_\alpha^\dagger,\hat c_\beta^\dagger,\hat c_\delta,\hat c_\gamma,\hat\beta_{\nu_1}^\dagger(\Omega),\ldots,\hat\beta_{\nu_{r_2}}^\dagger(\Omega)\right). \end{aligned}\)

转动后的密度与各种收缩结构

跃迁密度与配对密度

\[\rho_{\alpha\beta}(\Omega)=\frac{\langle\Phi|\hat c_\beta^\dagger\hat c_\alpha|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle}{n(\Omega)},\qquad\kappa_{\alpha\beta}(\Omega)=\frac{\langle\Phi|\hat c_\beta\hat c_\alpha|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle}{n(\Omega)},\qquad\bar\kappa_{\alpha\beta}(\Omega)=\frac{\langle\Phi|\hat c_\beta^\dagger\hat c_\alpha^\dagger|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle}{n(\Omega)}.\]
\[\rho(\Omega)=V^*(\Omega)\mathcal A^{-1}(\Omega)V^T,\qquad\kappa(\Omega)=V^*(\Omega)\mathcal A^{-1}(\Omega)U^T,\qquad\bar\kappa(\Omega)=U^*(\Omega)\mathcal A^{-1}(\Omega)V^T.\]

其他收缩

\[\langle\Phi|\hat\beta_\mu\hat\beta_\nu|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle,\qquad\langle\Phi|\hat\beta_\mu\hat\beta_\nu^\dagger(\Omega)|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle,\qquad\langle\Phi|\hat\beta_\mu^\dagger(\Omega)\hat\beta_\nu^\dagger(\Omega)|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle.\]
\[\langle\Phi|\hat\beta_\mu\hat c_\alpha|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle,\qquad\langle\Phi|\hat\beta_\mu\hat c_\alpha^\dagger|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle,\qquad\langle\Phi|\hat c_\alpha\hat\beta_\nu^\dagger(\Omega)|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle,\qquad\langle\Phi|\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat\beta_\nu^\dagger(\Omega)|\Phi(\Omega)\rangle.\]

多准粒子态之间的单体与两体矩阵元

单体算符矩阵元

\(\displaystyle \hat O^{(1)}=\sum_{\alpha\beta}O_{\alpha\beta}\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_\beta,\qquad O_{\alpha\beta}=\langle\alpha|\hat O|\beta\rangle=\int d\mathbf x\,\phi_\alpha^*(\mathbf x)O(\mathbf x)\phi_\beta(\mathbf x).\)
\(\displaystyle \hat{\boldsymbol s}=\left(\hat\beta_{\mu_{r_1}},\ldots,\hat\beta_{\mu_1},\hat c_\alpha^\dagger,\hat c_\beta,\hat\beta_{\nu_1}^\dagger(\Omega),\ldots,\hat\beta_{\nu_{r_2}}^\dagger(\Omega)\right).\)
\(\displaystyle \boxed{\langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|\hat O^{(1)}\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle=n(\Omega)\sum_{\alpha\beta}O_{\alpha\beta}\operatorname{pf}[S(\alpha,\beta;\Omega)].}\)

两体算符矩阵元

\(\displaystyle \hat O^{(2)}=\frac12\sum_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}O^{(2)}_{\alpha\beta,\gamma\delta}\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_\beta^\dagger\hat c_\delta\hat c_\gamma,\qquad O^{(2)}_{\alpha\beta,\gamma\delta}=\langle\alpha\beta|\hat O|\gamma\delta\rangle.\)
\(\displaystyle O^{(2)}_{\alpha\beta,\gamma\delta}=\int d\mathbf x_1\,d\mathbf x_2\,\phi_\alpha^*(\mathbf x_1)\phi_\beta^*(\mathbf x_2)O(\mathbf x_1,\mathbf x_2)\phi_\gamma(\mathbf x_1)\phi_\delta(\mathbf x_2).\)
\(\displaystyle \hat{\boldsymbol s}=\left(\hat\beta_{\mu_{r_1}},\ldots,\hat\beta_{\mu_1},\hat c_\alpha^\dagger,\hat c_\beta^\dagger,\hat c_\delta,\hat c_\gamma,\hat\beta_{\nu_1}^\dagger(\Omega),\ldots,\hat\beta_{\nu_{r_2}}^\dagger(\Omega)\right).\)
\(\displaystyle \boxed{\langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|\hat O^{(2)}\hat R(\Omega)|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle=\frac{n(\Omega)}2\sum_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}O^{(2)}_{\alpha\beta,\gamma\delta}\operatorname{pf}[S(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta;\Omega)].}\)

投影壳模型哈密顿量

哈密顿量的组成

\(\displaystyle \hat H=\hat H_0-\frac{\chi_{QQ}}2\sum_{\mu=-2}^{2}\hat Q_{2\mu}^{\dagger}\hat Q_{2\mu}-G_M\hat P^\dagger\hat P-G_Q\sum_{\mu=-2}^{2}\hat P_{2\mu}^{\dagger}\hat P_{2\mu}.\)
\(\displaystyle \hat H_0=\sum_{\alpha\beta}\langle\alpha|\hat h_{\mathrm{sph}}|\beta\rangle\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_\beta=\sum_k\epsilon_k\hat c_k^\dagger\hat c_k.\)
\(\displaystyle \hat h_{\mathrm{sph}}=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2+\frac12m\omega_{00}^{\,2}r^2-2\hbar\omega_{00}\kappa\,\boldsymbol s\cdot\boldsymbol l-\hbar\omega_{00}\kappa\mu\,\boldsymbol l^2.\)

四极与配对算符

\(\displaystyle \hat Q_{2\mu}=\sum_{\alpha\beta}\langle\alpha|\hat Q_{2\mu}|\beta\rangle\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_\beta.\)
\(\displaystyle \hat P^\dagger=\frac12\sum_\alpha\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_{\bar\alpha}^\dagger,\qquad\hat P_{2\mu}^\dagger=\frac12\sum_{\alpha\beta}\langle\alpha|\hat Q_{2\mu}|\beta\rangle\hat c_\alpha^\dagger\hat c_{\bar\beta}^\dagger.\)

投影壳模型中的不可约张量算符

不可约张量算符的转动与投影算符

\(\displaystyle \hat R^\dagger(\Omega)\hat T_{\lambda\mu}\hat R(\Omega)=\sum_\nu D^{\lambda *}_{\mu\nu}(\Omega)\hat T_{\lambda\nu},\qquad[\hat N,\hat T_{\lambda\mu}]=0.\)
\(\displaystyle \begin{aligned} \hat P^{I_1\dagger}_{M_1K_1}\hat T_{\lambda\mu}&=\frac{2I_1+1}{16\pi^2}\int d\Omega\,D^{I_1}_{M_1K_1}(\Omega)\hat R^\dagger(\Omega)\hat T_{\lambda\mu}\\ &=\sum_\nu\frac{2I_1+1}{16\pi^2}\int d\Omega\,D^{I_1}_{M_1K_1}(\Omega)D^{\lambda *}_{\mu\nu}(\Omega)\hat T_{\lambda\nu}\hat R^\dagger(\Omega)\\ &=\sum_{IM\nu}(-1)^{\mu-\nu}\langle I_1M_1;\lambda,-\mu|IM\rangle\langle I_1K_1;\lambda,-\nu|I,K_1-\nu\rangle\frac{2I_1+1}{2I+1}\,\hat T_{\lambda\nu}\hat P^{I\dagger}_{M,K_1-\nu}. \end{aligned}\)

不可约张量的矩阵元

\(\displaystyle \begin{aligned} &\langle\Phi^{I_1N}_{M_1,K_1;\kappa_1}|\hat T_{\lambda\mu}|\Phi^{I_2N}_{M_2,K_2;\kappa_2}\rangle=\langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|\hat P^{I_1\dagger}_{M_1K_1}\hat T_{\lambda\mu}\hat P^{I_2}_{M_2K_2}\hat P^N|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle\\ &=\langle I_2M_2;\lambda\mu|I_1M_1\rangle\sum_\nu\langle I_2,K_1-\nu;\lambda\nu|I_1K_1\rangle\langle\Phi_{\kappa_1}|\hat T_{\lambda\nu}\hat P^{I_2}_{K_1-\nu,K_2}\hat P^N|\Phi_{\kappa_2}\rangle\\ &=\frac{\langle I_2M_2;\lambda\mu|I_1M_1\rangle}{\sqrt{2I_1+1}}\langle\Phi^{I_1N}_{K_1;\kappa_1}\Vert\hat T_\lambda\Vert\Phi^{I_2N}_{K_2;\kappa_2}\rangle. \end{aligned}\)

投影壳模型结合耦合道的初步想法

耦合道方法简述

波函数： \(\displaystyle \Psi_{JM}(\boldsymbol r_\alpha)=\sum_c a^{(c)}\Psi_{JM}^{(c)}(\boldsymbol r_\alpha)=\sum_c a^{(c)}\frac{u_c(r_\alpha)}{r_\alpha}\bigl[Y_l(\hat{\boldsymbol r}_\alpha)\otimes\Phi_I(D)\bigr]_{JM}.\)
通道势场： \(\displaystyle V_{cc'}^{JM}(r_\alpha)=\left\langle\bigl[Y_l(\hat{\boldsymbol r}_\alpha)\otimes\Phi_I(D)\bigr]_{JM}\middle|\hat V(\boldsymbol r_\alpha)\middle|\bigl[Y_{l'}(\hat{\boldsymbol r}_\alpha)\otimes\Phi_{I'}(D)\bigr]_{JM}\right\rangle.\)
约化矩阵元展开： \(\displaystyle V_{cc'}^{JM}(r_\alpha)=\sum_\lambda(-1)^{l'+I+J}\begin{Bmatrix}l&I&J\\I'&l'&\lambda\end{Bmatrix}\langle l\Vert Y_\lambda\Vert l'\rangle\langle\Phi_I(D)\Vert\hat V_\lambda(r_\alpha)\Vert\Phi_{I'}(D)\rangle.\)
多通道薛定谔方程： \(\displaystyle \boxed{\sum_{c'}\left[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu}\left(\frac{d^2}{dr_\alpha^2}-\frac{l(l+1)}{r_\alpha^2}\right)\delta_{cc'}+V_{cc'}^{JM}(r_\alpha)\right]u_{c'}(r_\alpha)=E_cu_c(r_\alpha).}\)

投影壳模型结合方案1

\(\displaystyle \hat V_{\lambda\mu}^{C}(r_\alpha)=\frac{Z_\alpha e}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{4\pi}{2\lambda+1}\frac{\hat Q_{\lambda\mu}^{(D)}}{r_\alpha^{\lambda+1}},\qquad\langle\Phi_{IK}\Vert\hat V_\lambda^{C}(r_\alpha)\Vert\Phi_{I'K'}\rangle=\frac{Z_\alpha e}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{4\pi}{2\lambda+1}\frac{\langle\Phi_{IK}\Vert\hat Q_\lambda^{(D)}\Vert\Phi_{I'K'}\rangle}{r_\alpha^{\lambda+1}}.\)

投影壳模型结合方案2

\(\displaystyle \langle\Psi^{I_1N}\Vert\hat Q_\lambda\Vert\Psi^{I_2N}\rangle=\sum_{\kappa_1K_1,\kappa_2K_2}f_{\kappa_1K_1}^{I_1*}f_{\kappa_2K_2}^{I_2}\langle\Phi^{I_1N}_{K_1;\kappa_1}\Vert\hat Q_\lambda\Vert\Phi^{I_2N}_{K_2;\kappa_2}\rangle.\)
